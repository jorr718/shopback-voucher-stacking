The voucher side specifically refers to the **gift cards and digital vouchers** you can buy directly inside the ShopBack app. These are prepaid instruments for popular brands — restaurants, grocery stores, travel booking sites, retail chains — and the platform pays you instant cashback on the purchase price of the card itself. This is distinct from the broader cashback flow where you click through to a merchant and earn a percentage back on whatever you spend there. Vouchers are a prepaid layer — you commit to the brand upfront, and the brand rewards that commitment with a higher cashback rate than you'd typically get on a standard click-through purchase.

## How Vouchers Stack With Promo Codes and Credit Card Rewards

This is the part most people get wrong, and it's where the real savings live.

**The three-layer stack.** A single purchase can carry three independent discounts, each funded by a different party:

- **Layer 1 — ShopBack cashback**, funded by the merchant's affiliate commission. You trigger this by clicking through from ShopBack before checkout.
- **Layer 2 — the merchant's own promo code**, funded by the merchant's marketing budget. You enter this in the promo code field at checkout.
- **Layer 3 — your credit card rewards**, funded by your card issuer. You earn points, miles, or cashback on the final charged amount.

All three land on the same order in most cases. The order matters, and the only hard rule is that you must start at ShopBack. Here's the stacking flow that works:

1. Open ShopBack (app or web) and search for the merchant.
2. Tap "Shop Now" to click through — this sets the tracking cookie.
3. Add items to your cart on the merchant site. Don't open a second tab or click a different affiliate link in between.
4. Apply the merchant's promo code in the promo code field at checkout. Confirm the discount hits the order total.
5. Complete the order in the same session, paying with your rewards credit card.
6. Cashback tracks against your ShopBack account as Pending, then moves to Confirmed after the return window closes.

A small number of merchants exclude specific promo-code-driven orders from cashback — usually deep influencer codes that already carry a heavy commission split. That exclusion is disclosed on the store's ShopBack page, so if you're stacking a very aggressive code, read the terms before you check out.

**Where vouchers fit in the stack.** Gift card vouchers add a fourth layer when you use them as the payment method. You buy the voucher through ShopBack and earn instant cashback on the purchase, then you apply the voucher at checkout on top of any promo code discount, and you still earn your credit card rewards on the voucher purchase itself. The math gets interesting fast — a 10% voucher cashback plus a 15% promo code plus 2% card rewards on a $100 order can push total savings past 25% without any single layer doing heavy lifting.

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

**Mistake 1 — starting at the merchant instead of ShopBack.** This is the single most common reason cashback doesn't track. The referral has to originate from ShopBack; if you land on the merchant through a Google search or a social media ad, no referral, no cashback. Fix: bookmark shopback.com or keep the app on your home screen, and start there every time.

**Mistake 2 — clicking a coupon site between ShopBack and checkout.** Even if you started at ShopBack, detouring to a coupon aggregator to "check for better codes" overwrites the tracking cookie and breaks the referral. Fix: use only the codes ShopBack surfaces on the merchant page — those are merchant-approved and cashback-compatible.

**Mistake 3 — using ad-blockers on the merchant checkout page.** Aggressive ad-blockers and privacy extensions can strip the tracking signal. Fix: disable ad-blockers on the merchant's checkout domain, or use a fresh browser session for ShopBack-driven purchases.

**Mistake 4 — counting Pending cashback as money in the bank.** Pending cashback can reverse if the order is returned or cancelled. Fix: only count Confirmed cashback toward your withdrawal planning, and wait for the merchant's return window to close before treating it as yours.

**Mistake 5 — ignoring the gift card layer.** A lot of users only use the click-through cashback flow and miss that the gift card marketplace often carries 3–5x higher rates for the same brands. Fix: before any planned purchase at a brand in the voucher directory, check whether buying a gift card first earns more than clicking through directly.
